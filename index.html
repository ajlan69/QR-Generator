Free Custom QR Code Generator

Design a QR code with your own shapes, colors, logo and frame, then download it as PNG or SVG. No account needed.

What is a QR code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores a web address, text or contact detail. A phone camera reads it and opens what is encoded.

How to create one

  1. Enter a URL or text.
  2. Pick a quick style, or adjust shapes, colors, logo and frame.
  3. Scan the preview with your phone.
  4. Save as PNG or SVG.

What it's good for

Links, menus, event pages, social profiles, contact info, product pages and posters.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account is needed to create or download QR codes.

Is my content uploaded?

No. The QR code, including any logo you add, is made in your browser.

Will a styled QR code still scan?

Usually, if there's strong contrast between the code and background and you leave a quiet zone. Dark on light scans best, so avoid inverted colors. Always test the final code with a phone before printing.

Why does a logo change the error correction?

A logo covers part of the code. High error correction lets scanners recover that missing data.
